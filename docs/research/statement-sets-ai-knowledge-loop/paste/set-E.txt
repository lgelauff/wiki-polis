Wikimedia should publish machine-readable signals about the editorial quality and review state of its content.
Wikimedia should develop Wikidata as the primary structured knowledge layer that AI systems draw on.
Wikimedia should fund a technical environment for AI experimentation that is separate from production infrastructure.
Wikimedia's production infrastructure is poorly suited to rapid experimentation with AI tools.
A state-of-the-art open AI model is a prerequisite for the rest of Wikimedia's AI strategy.
Wikimedia should contribute training data and reciprocity standards to public AI models built by partners, rather than training a model itself.
Wikimedia should withhold its name from AI models whose behaviour it cannot control.
Wikimedia should develop Wikimedia Enterprise into the main channel through which AI systems reach its content.
Wikimedia should recruit content partners specifically to supply data that AI systems currently lack.
Wikimedia should fund crowdsourced collection of scarce data types, such as recordings of spoken language.
Wikimedia should prioritise AI tools that support existing editors over tools that serve readers.
Building AI tools for editors will accelerate the decline in the number of active editors.
Wikimedia should adopt machine learning models built by others rather than developing that capacity in-house.
Wikimedia's AI technology roadmap should follow the Global Majority Technology Priorities set in 2024.
