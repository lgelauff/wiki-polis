No single Wikimedia organisation can carry out an AI strategy for the movement on its own.
The Wikimedia Foundation should lead the movement's response to AI.
A coordinating body spanning movement entities, volunteers, and outside partners should be established for AI work.
Creating another movement-wide coordinating body will produce meetings rather than results.
The Wikimedia Endowment should provide seed funding for the movement's AI work.
AI work should be funded jointly by several organisations, so that no single funder sets its direction.
Movement organisations should fund AI work from core budgets rather than through grants.
Smaller Wikimedia affiliates can move faster on AI experimentation than the largest organisations.
Wikimedia should partner with public AI developers such as Apertus, OLMo, and Pleias.
Partnering with named AI developers would compromise Wikimedia's standing as a neutral source.
Movement leaders should take public positions in external debates about AI governance.
Wikimedia should present a position on AI governance at the 2027 Geneva AI Summit.
Wikimedia CH should open space for the movement to act in Switzerland rather than lead the work itself.
Wikimedia should spend the next two years building agreement across the movement before committing to a technical direction.
