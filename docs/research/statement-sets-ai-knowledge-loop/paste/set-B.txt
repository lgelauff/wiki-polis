Wikimedia's current approach to AI companies is closer to passive acceptance than to active negotiation.
Rate-limiting AI crawlers protects Wikimedia infrastructure at an acceptable cost to openness.
Restricting access to Wikimedia content in response to AI scraping would do more damage to the mission than the scraping does.

The volume of openly shared content is no longer an adequate measure of the health of the knowledge commons.

Taking an active political stance toward AI companies would damage Wikimedia's reputation for neutrality.
