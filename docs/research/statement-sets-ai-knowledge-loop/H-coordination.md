## Notes

**H-14 is the timing objection, and it is the one most likely to actually happen.** The paper's closing argument is urgency: "the window will not remain open indefinitely." The movement's habitual response to a bold proposal is consultation. H-14 states that response as a legitimate position rather than as inertia, which it sometimes is and sometimes is not. Its agreement level against A-06 (the Stack Overflow trajectory) is the most informative cross-set pairing in the extraction: high agreement on both means the movement believes the threat and intends to deliberate anyway.

**H-02 and H-04 are the institutional-politics statements.** The paper is careful never to say who leads — it names WMF and WMDE as holding "core infrastructure and engineering capacity" and leaves authority undefined, then notes that cluster governance "will require dedicated attention." That evasion is understandable in a coalition-building document and unhelpful for planning. These two statements make the question askable.

**H-04 is a cynical statement, deliberately included.** Set H is otherwise a list of sensible-sounding governance items that will draw broad soft agreement and produce weak clustering. H-04 gives the sceptical position somewhere to land, and the correlation between H-04 and H-03 will show whether support for the cluster is genuine or polite.

**H-13 reproduces a distinction the paper draws carefully about itself.** Wikimedia CH's role "is not to lead the mission, but to open the space in which the movement can act." Since the paper is commissioned by Wikimedia CH, this is the one place where the document's own institutional position is on the ballot. Worth keeping for that reason alone.

**H-10 narrows B-12.** B-12 asks about political stance in general; H-10 asks about the specific reputational cost of naming partners. Related, not duplicative — a person can accept advocacy in the abstract and object to endorsing named vendors. Flagged in the cross-set duplicate check and retained.
